A military laser rifle for ZED ZONE.

[b]Adds[/b]
[list]
[*] Laser Rifle - full-auto laser, about 300 shots a minute, 400 damage per shot
[/list]
[b]Runs on Laser Battery Packs.[/b] Each pack holds 2000 energy, about 50 shots. Press R to swap a fresh pack from your inventory into the rifle, and recharge empty packs in the game's battery chargers. Packs turn up in the world and can be crafted.
The rifle can be crafted and turns up rarely in buildings.

[b]Crafting[/b]
[list]
[*] Laser Rifle: 2 Iron Wire, 2 Copper Wire, 2 Copper Tubes, 6 Metal Components
[*] Laser Battery Pack: 8 Copper Wire, 3 Copper Plates
[/list]

[b]How it fights[/b]
[list]
[*] The shot travels at 1200 m/s, so it lands the moment you pull the trigger
[*] A bright red beam flashes from the emitter to the target
[*] Every hit has a 15% chance to set the target on fire (uses the game's own burning, so water and dodging put it out)
[*] Shots that hit grass or wood have a small chance to start a fire there
[*] Almost no recoil and a tight spread
[*] A sharp electric crack on every shot
[/list]

In co-op, other players see your beams and hear the shots. Everyone needs the mod.

Every number (damage, fire chance, beam colour and width, sound volume) can be changed in BepInEx\config\girthy_mods.zedzone.lasergun.cfg. Want a different sound? Drop any WAV named fire.wav into the mod's LaserGun folder.

Needs BepInEx (IL2CPP) with the ZED ZONE mod loader. Log: BepInEx\LaserGun.log

[b]Settings and keys:[/b] Press [b]F11[/b] in game to open [b]Mod Menu[/b] (Steam installs it with this mod) to change this mod's settings without editing files.
